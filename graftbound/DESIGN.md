# GRAFTBOUND — design summary (v0.5)
**Loop:**
1. Act map node: battle, elite, Wayshrine or boss.
2. Tactical battle on an 8×8 HD-2D board.
3. Booster of 3 real cards.
4. Graft a creature (capacity 4/6/8 per act) or inscribe/empower a spell.
5. Next node. There are 3 acts × 8 nodes.

**Grafting:** additive with real printed stats. A duplicate ability gives Resonance (+2 Spark). Traits come from abilities AND creature types. Health = Spark 6 + Toughness.

**Wounds:** a victory heals half of your missing health. Act bosses and Restoration heal fully.

**Spells:** reach the whole battlefield; mana-limited; once per turn each. Every 3 spells in the spellbook give +1 max mana and +1 regen.

**Wayshrines** offer up to 4 of 6 offerings: Restoration, Spark Font (max 3 per run), Offering Bowl (costs a wound), Graftsmith (sever a graft, then pick from a stronger creature pack), Arcane Font (max 2), Inscription Altar.

**Acts:**
- **I — Forgotten Hollow:** grubs, hounds, archers, wardens, moths, hexers; elite Alpha; boss Thornmother.
- **II — Ashen Warrens (lava):** Cinderlings that burst, Armored Brutes, Skyhook Harpooners that ground fliers, Emberseers with area blasts; elite Obsidian Champion; boss Pyre Matron (cross breath, lava creation, broods).
- **III — Fractured Nexus (rifts):** Rift Stalkers that teleport behind you, Null Wardens that tax spells, Shardlings; elite Nexus Sentinel; boss the Unwritten, who copies your abilities.
