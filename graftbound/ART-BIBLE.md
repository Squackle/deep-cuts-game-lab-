# GRAFTBOUND art bible (v0.5)

- **Identity:** GRAFTBOUND wordmark plus an original sigil (a card-shaped seal bound by a vine). Palette: forest green, parchment and brass. Act accents: ember orange (Warrens) and violet (Nexus).
- **Status:** all sprites (21 sheets), 19 evolution layers, 16 terrain materials, 11 foliage types, the booster wrapper and all VFX are **original programmer art**. They are placeholder quality and the target of a dedicated art pass.
- **Terrain:**
  - 32 px cells in a 4×4 atlas, plus a pre-filtered blur atlas built at asset time.
  - Two calm stones per cell with bevels, grout, sparse cracks and moss.
  - Dither amplitude was reduced from v0.4.
- **Sprites:** 48×48 player frames; front and back views × idle 4 / walk 4 / attack 4 / cast 3 / hurt 2. Authored facing left, light from the upper left.
- **Booster:** original SVG wrapper (brass filigree corners, vine pattern, central sigil medallion, wax seal, foil sheen). It deliberately does not resemble an official Magic booster.
- **Real-card images** come from Scryfall, unmodified, and are only ever shown as card faces.
