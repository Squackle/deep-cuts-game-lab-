    RULES IN FOCUS

    IT'S NOT A SECOND CAST.

    Twincast copies the original spell onto the stack. The copy inherits its modes, chosen targets and other cast-time choices. You may change legal targets for that copy when it is made.

    Redirect changes the target of an existing spell. Its mode cannot change. The spell's own controller determines target legality.

    For multi-target spells, each slot matters. Two Arc Trail targets must remain different.
