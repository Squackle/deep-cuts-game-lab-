## Completed patch

Previously, unavailable Scryfall metadata left anonymous blank cards; cached URLs could also fail independently. `src/card-fallback.js` renders escaped names, costs, local/fetched printed text and creature stats. `src/ui.js` replaces a failed image without resetting game state or drag/target selection. `styles.css` gives the text fallback a matching forest/gold style and usable land-button height. `build.py` includes the renderer; `index.html` is regenerated.

Rules, payment handling, scenario definitions, daily manifest, saved progress and ranked bridge messages are unchanged. Main-site impact: NONE until a separately reviewed deployment/promotion.

## Difficulty finding — unresolved

All 250 classic reference routes have existing passing tests. However, exhaustive ordered subsets find shorter winning response lines in **61/250** stages; `sb-07` uses one response instead of its reference two. This is evidence against treating reference counts as minimum difficulty, not a reason to forbid efficient play. Witnesses and an explicit bounded decision-graph gate live in the Website audit. Only 81/250 full graphs finish at the current 1,500-state budget; others are inconclusive. Both campaigns fail the proposed progression gate.

A future ladder needs constrained target/payment/order choices that interact, distinct solution families, counterexamples to greedy play and human difficulty calibration. Adding independent threats or a fixed response quota alone does not demonstrate richer reasoning. No artificial difficulty score or extra mandatory actions were added here.

No remote player data, production D1, game version or deployment changed. Remote CI results belong on the review PR. Rollback is a source revert plus regeneration of `index.html`; no data migration.
