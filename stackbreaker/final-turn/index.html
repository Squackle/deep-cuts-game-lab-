    Mission

    Break the stack, then win. Your opponent has 3 life. You're at 2, facing a lethal Lightning Bolt. Respond before passing. After the crisis, advance into your own turn and finish the game.

    Need a hint?

    Tap both Islands to cast Counterspell targeting Lightning Bolt. Pass to resolve it. After the opponent's end step, advance through your upkeep and draw to your main phase, then tap your Mountain, cast Lava Spike at the opponent and resolve it.

    Phase 1 supports a real continuing stack/priority window, turn advance, untap, draw, main-phase sorcery timing and a game-ending lethal spell. Combat, blockers, opponent AI and general card mechanics are not implemented yet.
