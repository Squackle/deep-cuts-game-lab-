    RULES IN FOCUS

    WARD IS NOT HEXPROOF.

    Ward triggers when an opponent targets a ward permanent with a spell or ability. The original target is legal. The ward ability goes on the stack above that spell.

    When ward resolves, the spell or ability’s controller may pay the listed cost. Otherwise ward counters the spell or ability. Stifle can counter that triggered ability.

    Triggered abilities can remain on the stack even if their source leaves. When multiple triggers occur, the active player stacks theirs first (APNAP).
