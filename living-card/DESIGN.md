# GRAFTBOUND — Design (v0.4)
*Begin as nothing. Become everything.*

**Core loop:** map node → turn-based tactical battle (8×8 HD-2D board) → three-card booster → **graft** a creature or inscribe/empower a spell → next node → the Thornmother.

**Grafting:** additive and deterministic. 1/1 + 2/2 deathtouch = 3/3 deathtouch; + 3/2 flying = 6/5 deathtouch + flying. A duplicate ability becomes Resonance (+1/+1). Every graft adds visible traits from its abilities **and** creature type (Angel → wings and aura, Knight → armor and crown, …), arranged in 5 readable slots. Health = Spark (5) + 2 × toughness.

**Cards:** real Magic cards by default (37-card curated, rules-checked pool, adapted and labeled), plus original cards for mechanics the pool lacks. The original set alone is available in Settings.

**Combat:** move, one attack, and any number of different spells per turn. 14 abilities, 12 spell effects, 8 enemy types (incl. the Briar Hexer), and a Legendary boss with a strike cap and a telegraphed, health-scaled slam. Strong builds draw reinforcements; enemy health is never inflated.

**Progression:** no daily resets. Permanent achievements, unlocks, Codex, records and settings; each adventure starts from a fresh Blank.
