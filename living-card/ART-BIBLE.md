# GRAFTBOUND art bible (v0.4)
- **Identity:** GRAFTBOUND wordmark in a display serif with a parchment-to-brass gradient, "BOUND" in brass, and an original sigil (a card-shaped seal bound by a thorned vine). Palette: forest green #0d1a14–#2c5236, parchment #ecdfbf, brass #c9a24a/#e9c972.
- **Status:** all sprites, layers, terrain and foliage are original **programmer pixel art (placeholders)**. 1 original illustrated card (Nightcap Toad). Real cards show unmodified Scryfall images when online and are never turned into sprites.
- **Specs:** player 48×48 frames; sheets = view (front, back) × anim (idle 4, walk 4, attack 4, cast 3, hurt 2); authored facing left; 1/19 world unit per texel; light from the upper left; ≤24 colors per character.
- **Evolution slots:** back (wings>shell>spikes), body (armor), head (crown>horns>antlers), rear (tail), front (blades>claws>tusks>beak); ≤2 surface effects; ≤3 color markings.
