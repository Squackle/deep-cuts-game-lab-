# GRAFTBOUND playtest checklist (for the owner)
Open `<preview-url>/living-card/` on desktop AND on your phone.

1. Title + intro look right? Tap "Enter the Hollow".
2. First battle: move (tap a tile twice on phone), attack the grubs, end turn.
3. Booster: open the pack, reveal all, pick the 2/2 deathtouch creature → you should become 3/3 and the sprite should change.
4. Next battle: deathtouch should kill anything it damages.
5. Refresh the page mid-battle → "Continue Adventure" should resume exactly where you were.
6. Try a spell card, a Wayshrine, an Elite, and (if you get there) the Thornmother.

Note anything that felt confusing, too easy or too hard, slow on your phone, or ugly. Settings → Graphics → Performance if it stutters.
