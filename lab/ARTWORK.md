# Shared Game Lab cover art

The three first-party illustrations in `lab/covers/` are original SVG artworks based on the games' own mechanics and the site's dark-fantasy aesthetic. They do not embed, fetch, or hotlink Wizards of the Coast card art, official mana icons, Scryfall images, or outside image providers.

- **Hive Run** — an original armored, evolving brood-creature above luminous eggs in a green cavern. The art signals survival, encounters, and growing swarms.
- **Stackbreaker** — a caster interacting with floating cards and blue/violet spell energy, signaling priority, spells, and stack order.
- **Mana Heist** — a five-seal vault door with glowing resource stones, signaling mana decisions and successive puzzle wards.

These are **decorative cover illustrations**, not game screenshots or promises of in-game assets. UI descriptions remain accurate to existing playable builds. Images are hosted directly by Cloudflare Pages and have meaningful alternative descriptions, fixed dimensions, and no JavaScript.

## Shared Lab navigation

All three game headers use a small, keyboard-accessible `← ALL GAMES` action resolving to `/lab/`. Hive Run retains its root game `/` and its optional Game Only view, Stackbreaker retains its self-contained bundled `index.html` and rebuild source, and Mana Heist retains its existing map and game states. No local progress, scoring engine, or public website links are modified beyond clarifying the Lab return destination.

This artwork and catalog update does **not** promote any game to the public Deep Cuts EDH Arcade. Future gameplay changes remain in separate game-owned branches.
