Design and release requirements

Commander

Choose one commander before wave 1. Commanders fight on the road, block one ground enemy and return 20 seconds after falling. Passives work only while your commander is alive.

Wave forecast

★ Mana Spring: +35% farm output. ▲ High Ground: +30 range. Call another wave whenever combat is active, even while enemies are spawning. The bonus falls from 40 mana to zero over the previous wave’s bonus window. A wave must begin spawning before its successor earns a bonus. Waves do not start automatically. Enemy fliers bypass ground troops. Bosses and Titans stop for whoever blocks them and smash every ground troop in reach. They cannot be rooted, and slows affect them less.
