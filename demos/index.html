HOW TO TRAP PREY.

A short guided training video showing four ordinary captures, two mutation choices, and the Burrow Stalker miniboss. The actual Hive Run renderer and game rules are used in these practice scenes.

Important: This is an assisted, staged practice demonstration, not a continuous human-played or legitimate ranked run. The enemy and body positions were reset between scenarios to make the trapping mechanic easy to see. Hive Run is currently endless; there is no final ending screen yet.

What to watch for

  • Build a U-shaped body wall. Prevent the prey from escaping forward or to either side, rather than chasing directly behind it.
  • Keep your own head's escape lane clear. A successful trap should not cost you the run.
  • Four regular captures can unlock the first miniboss. Each regular capture gives 3 evolution energy. Every 5 energy buys a mutation, and the Burrow Stalker arrives after the second completed mutation.
  • The Burrow Stalker takes two captures. Its first trap changes the phase; only the final one awards the miniboss reward.
