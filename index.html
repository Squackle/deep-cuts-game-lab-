    FIELD GUIDE · HOW TO PLAY & ENCOUNTERS

    PREDATOR HUNT: Trap prey using your body. Your first ordinary-prey collision is forgiven and early enemies are slower. Bosses need two or three captures.

    BROOD TRAIL: Classic Snake. Swipe or use arrow keys / WASD, collect brood orbs, grow your swarm and avoid your tail.

    MUTATIONS: Captures grant 3 energy; Brood Trail orbs grant 1. Every 5 energy, select with 1 / 2 / 3 or tap a card (WASD and arrows never choose). Five original WUBRG powers combine: White + Green unlocks a Hatchling Ward (press Q or its button, consuming one shield and one growth charge); Blue + Red grants extra steering time; Black + Green can save a tail segment on a molt.

    BOSSES: Evolution 2: Burrow Stalker; 4: Hatchery Warden; 6: Tunnel Weaver; 8: Rift Guardian; 12: Broodmother (Egg Bloom). Its eggs hatch into slow-moving obstacles after a warning, and the Broodmother itself moves more slowly. Boss dossiers pause the action before encounters. All five can be practiced in The Lair.

    DEPTHS: Backgrounds change after 4, 8, and 12 evolutions. Dashed tiles warn before blocking.

    PATCH NOTES.

    Latest changes to the standalone test build. PROFILE CODEX PREVIEW ↗

    15 OCT 2026 · MATRIARCH

    Matriarch

    TEST ONLY: Three-capture slower Broodmother at evolution 12 and in The Lair; Egg Bloom now hatches slow crawling obstacles instead of becoming temporary stone, White+Green Hatchling Ward (Q or tap), Blue+Red and Black+Green mutation synergies. None are on the public game yet.

    15 OCT 2026 · BEACON

    Beacon

    Safer orb pickups: mutation choices use 1 / 2 / 3 or tap only, never movement keys. Improved amber escape cues and capture score flashes in the normal arcade game. Hive Codex is previewed separately as a profile feature—not an extra game HUD. Beacon 1.4 shipped separately on the public site.

    14 OCT 2026 · SPINDLE

    Spindle

    Webs recharge every 5 seconds, with three simultaneously. Rift Guardian charges for 4 seconds. Bosses avoid their own ability hazards. Phase and ability meters are outside the arena.

    14 OCT 2026 · SNARE

    Snare

    Webs immobilize only your swarm while the Weaver keeps hunting and spinning webs. Rift Guardian pulses every 2 seconds; all bosses move at the same cadence. Brood Trail is Arcade-only, and the dated challenge has been retired.

    14 OCT 2026 · LAIR

    Lair

    Select and practice any of the four bosses. Instant retry, danger warnings and richer capture effects. No leaderboard or profile scores.

    14 OCT 2026 · INSTINCT

    Instinct

    Mutation shortcuts: W/A/S, ↑/←/↓, or 1/2/3. Desktop gameplay waits for a deliberate movement key; touch play resumes instantly. Brood Trail descriptions now match its orb-only rules. HUD shows a session-only best.

    14 OCT 2026 · UI CLEANUP

    Clearer Playfield

    Removed directional buttons, extra statistics and the long instruction sidebar. Steering remains swipe and keyboard, with hidden diagnostics available on demand.

    14 OCT 2026 · SOLO LAB

    Local Experiments Only

    Removed shared scores, account links and submissions. Test both modes locally with Arcade runs and repeatable Lair encounters.

    14 OCT 2026 · 1.0.4

    Four Painted Depths

    Added individually illustrated backgrounds for the Hatchery, Burrows, Fracture and Deep Fracture.

    14 OCT 2026 · 1.0.3

    Boss Dossiers

    Each boss pauses the game for a mechanics briefing and a three-second regroup countdown.

    14 OCT 2026 · 1.0.2

    Early Game Balance

    Faster turn feedback, one forgiving prey collision, slower opening enemies, quicker orbs and optional trap hints.
