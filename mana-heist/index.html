For this puzzle system, each spell has a fixed cost, tap abilities make mana, and an end-turn action refreshes lands. You may Undo without penalty.

HELP · SCORING & RULES

All stages must be cast in order. The deadline is the last permitted turn. Your three-star medal uses the proven fewest turns and least life loss; extra clicks do not lower your medal. There are no time penalties or hidden randomness. Mana and land statuses are deterministic. This is a simplified original heist puzzle, not an MTG rules engine.
