How to play

ACTIVATE wells to gather limited energy. CONVERT it with prisms. ROTATE wires until a device can reach the seal. OPEN the vault when you have every required resource.

Every activation, rotation, conversion, and seal opening costs one move. Undo and Reset are free. A solved vault never becomes a loss; the move target is for the three-star medal.
