How it works

Tap a land to add mana. Tapped lands rotate sideways. Multicolor lands offer a color choice. Play at most one land each turn, spend mana to cast the ward-breaking spells, and end the turn to untap your lands. You can undo freely.

Three-star route

The solver scores the fewest turns and least life lost, not the number of taps. Your floating mana expires at the end of each turn. Future chapters unlock sequentially.
